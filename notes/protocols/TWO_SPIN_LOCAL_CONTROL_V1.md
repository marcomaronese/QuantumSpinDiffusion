# Two-spin independent-local control v1

Declared 2026-10-23 before any scientific validation or test observations for
this study. This is the next bounded E5 diagnosis, not a capacity sweep, a
diffusion-necessity experiment, or a physical-sampling benchmark.

## Fixed conditions and question

Can the existing shared-ancilla architectures improve joint reconstruction
over genuinely independent learned local channels at the selected final-only
objective? Keep j=1 on each spin, three steps, one layer, independent time
parameters, prior I9/9, forward D=1, dt=0.5. Compare exactly:

- `independent_local`: two distinct fresh |0> ancillas, one per spin, no
  coupling or shared randomness; eight local controls per spin (Jx,Jy,Jz,
  JxXa,JyYa,JzZa,IXa,IYa), 48 parameters total;
- `shared_no_direct`: the existing shared-ancilla ansatz without explicit
  inter-spin generators, 33 parameters;
- `shared_direct`: the existing shared-ancilla ansatz including Jz1Jz2 and
  Jx1Jx2+Jy1Jy2, 39 parameters.

Use the existing Hilbert--Schmidt normalization in the full collision space.
The local control factors analytically as E1 tensor E2 for arbitrary weights;
from a product prior its connected correlations and quantum MI must be zero
within 1e-10. Verify factorization numerically on every generated trajectory.
This is not the empirical product-of-marginals baseline. Different ancilla
counts, ranks, generator normalization and parameter counts preclude an
isolated causal claim about coupling or a resource-matched advantage claim.

Final-state infidelity only; Adam lr=0.04, betas=(0.9,0.999), eps=1e-8,
80 updates, no restarts/checkpoint selection/early stopping. CPU, one Torch
thread, deterministic algorithms, float64/complex128. Initialize all angles
as 0.03 standard normals. Independent local halves use separate RNG seeds;
the two shared models reuse named-generator angles exactly. Architecture
initializations are paired by role/name where applicable, not by tensor shape.
Record complete initial/final weights, histories, gradients, updates, times,
channel evaluations (three per update), dimensions and parameter counts.

## Fresh splits and fixed classical controls

Five training realizations use seeds 101,103,107,109,113, 1200 paired directions
each, sigma=0.18 and rotation=0.55. Validation: seed+110000, 1200 pairs.
Closed confirmation: seed+130000, 1200 pairs, not generated by the screen.
Shared initialization: seed+120000; local initialization uses seed+220000
and seed+320000 for its two halves. Paired datasets are the five statistical
units. No observations from the previous supervision study are reused.

Fit fixed baselines once per training realization: empirical factorized
marginals; tangent heat bootstrap (bandwidth .1); two-component separable
tensor mixture; 39-parameter neural moment generator (300 updates, lr=.03);
ambient Cartesian DDPM (300 epochs, 64 steps, batch256, width64, lr=.001).
Stochastic classical models use 2000 generated pairs; neural/DDPM/heat/tensor/
neural-sampling offsets are +140000/+150000/+160000/+170000/+180000.
Reuse these frozen outputs through validation and any confirmation. Target
and generated pairs use the same coherent encoding. Quantum states are exact;
classical sampled-state error and unequal budgets are explicit limitations.
There is no common finite physical POVM yet, so no sample/readout claim or MMD.

## Predeclared decision and guardrails

Primary metric: mean validation trace distance to independent validation
targets. Reference: `independent_local`. Eligible shared candidates satisfy
all physicality/channel and independent-replay checks and mean degradation
limits versus the reference: infidelity <=.005 and marginal trace distance
<=.01. Choose the eligible candidate with lowest mean trace distance;
ties prefer `shared_no_direct`. Proceed to confirmation only if it improves
trace distance by >=.01, connected-correlation error by >=.01, and is nonworse
in trace distance on >=4/5 training realizations. Improvement of joint error
without correlation improvement does not pass this correlation gate.

Numerical tolerances: trace/Hermiticity/positivity, Kraus completeness/Choi,
product residual and zero local-control correlation/MI <=1e-10; independent
NumPy/SciPy metric replay <=2e-9. Any invalid state/channel or replay failure
halts scientific selection. Save joint complex multipoles (Parseval check),
connected tensors, both marginals, global infidelity, Frobenius error,
quantum MI and its error. Source classical MI is not estimated and is not
interchangeable with quantum MI. Do not project states to fix invalid outputs.

Report per-realization effects and descriptive paired Student-t 95% intervals;
five seeds do not justify population, multiplicity-adjusted significance or
generalization claims. Baseline competitiveness is a separate descriptive
comparison. Retain all conditions/failures. A no-go leaves confirmation closed
and leads to a separately frozen diagnosis, not an automatic capacity sweep.

## Confirmation and artifact contract

If the screen passes, freeze its selected identity and the hashes of protocol,
selection, manifest and fitted output states before generating the closed
confirmation observations. Compare that same selected shared model to the
same learned independent-local model; include fixed baselines descriptively.
No retraining or selection on test. Use the same improvement thresholds,
4/5 nonworse rule and guardrails. A failed confirmation stays a failure; no
alternate candidate may be selected on these observations. Confirming five
fitted models on fresh observations does not create ten model realizations.

Run `.venv/bin/python -m tasks.two_spin_local_control` from the repository
root. Use a fresh `outputs/experiments/two_spin_local_control_v1/`; alternate
budgets/seeds require a new identity and are engineering checks, not v1 results.
Save the machine-readable protocol, exact source and checksums before data
creation. Save software/Git metadata, datasets/split hashes, encoded targets,
forward and reverse states, generators/parameters, baseline samples/models,
metrics, selection and a sealed artifact manifest. Independent replay is
`.venv/bin/python -m tasks.validate_two_spin_local_control`.
Confirmation is a separate invocation with `--confirm-from <screen-dir>` and
a fresh output directory, gated on a passing independently audited screen.
Never overwrite outputs or modify frozen paper evidence. These are working
results until a named evidence snapshot, ledger update and manuscript build.
